swarmkit / README.md
{{ dlStatus }}

swarmkit runs a real sub-agent: your local model (qwen2.5:1.5b) in a loop. It can request one real tool — fetch a URL or local file. Our code runs the tool, feeds the result back, and the loop repeats until the agent has its answer.

The thinking slider sets how many tool loops the agent may run. Low means one quick pass; max allows five rounds of fetch → read → revise. More loops give better answers on research tasks — and cost more tokens.

Cost. Every sub-agent run consumes tokens, scaled by the loop count. Plain chats (sub-agents off) stay free. You earn tokens back just by using your AI, and by completing quests.

Usage. After installing, a Sub-agents toggle appears above the chat composer. Turn it on for prompts that benefit from a source — e.g. "summarize https://example.com" or "read notes.txt and list the action items."

Good practice. Start at low loops and raise the slider only when the task genuinely needs more rounds. Watch your token balance in the top-right — running out means waiting to earn more.
